2026

Cyberport × DraperDragon Joint Accelerator Program (CDD-JAP)

Building AI & Web3 Ventures in Hong Kong

In 2026, CAI ecosystem leadership participated as a partner in building the Cyberport × DraperDragon Joint Accelerator Program (CDD-JAP), connecting emerging technology companies with Hong Kong's innovation infrastructure and global venture networks.

The program focuses on:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Web3
  • Digital Assets
  • Emerging Technologies

Key ecosystem connections:

  • Cyberport innovation ecosystem
  • DraperDragon venture network
  • Global investors and entrepreneurs
CREDENTIAL · CASE STUDY 02
2025

Hong Kong RWA & Digital Asset Immersion Program

Exploring Institutional Digital Asset Innovation

In 2025, CAI ecosystem partners organized an executive immersion program exploring Hong Kong's emerging Real World Asset (RWA) and digital asset ecosystem.

Participants included

  • Family offices
  • Listed company executives
  • Venture investors
  • Technology entrepreneurs

Key ecosystem visits

  • Hong Kong University innovation exchange
  • Cyberport ecosystem
  • Fosun International
  • CMB International
  • Amber Group
  • HKbitEX

Key outcomes:

  • Built Hong Kong RWA ecosystem map
  • Connected institutional investors and technology platforms
  • Explored compliant RWA commercialization opportunities
