WHY SUZHOU?

From Manufacturing Hub to Innovation Engine

Over the past three decades, Suzhou Industrial Park (SIP) has evolved from a manufacturing-oriented development zone into one of China's most dynamic innovation ecosystems.

Today, Suzhou integrates:

  • Artificial Intelligence and intelligent industries
  • Life science and biotechnology innovation
  • Venture capital and entrepreneurship
  • Global commercialization networks
