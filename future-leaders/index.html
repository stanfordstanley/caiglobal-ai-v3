CAI FUTURE LEADERS INITIATIVE

Developing the next generation of AI and innovation leaders

The CAI Future Leaders Initiative builds future-ready innovators through AI literacy, hands-on research and entrepreneurship, and direct exposure to CAI's cross-border ecosystem of universities, ventures, and institutions.

Powered by the Canada–Asia Council for AI and Digital Innovation

MISSION

Est. Framework — Articles I–III

ARTICLE I

AI Literacy

Building fluency in the technical and strategic trends defining the AI era — how the technology works, where it is heading, and what it means to build within it.

ARTICLE II

Innovation Experience

Direct participation in AI projects, applied research, and early-stage entrepreneurship — learning by building rather than by studying from a distance.

ARTICLE III

Global Exposure

Connection to CAI's network of universities, research institutions, and venture ecosystems across North America and Greater China.

PROGRAMS

Three tracks, one pipeline.

Each track draws directly on CAI's ecosystem relationships — not a standalone curriculum.

TRACK 01

Future Talent Strategy

A long-term growth pathway for young innovators and future entrepreneurs — oriented around direction and capability, not a single application cycle.

TRACK 02

AI & Research Experience

Structured entry into AI projects, research exposure, and entrepreneurial work, connected to CAI's venture studio and partner institutions.

TRACK 03

Global Innovation Mentorship

Direct mentorship from entrepreneurs, researchers, and ecosystem leaders across CAI's Canada–Asia network.

WHO THIS IS FOR

Families and innovators seeking a credible path.

A structured pathway into AI-era innovation — grounded in an existing network rather than a generic admissions program.

INTERNATIONAL FAMILIES

Evaluating long-term innovation and education pathways across North America and Greater China.

YOUNG INNOVATORS

Building an early AI or research portfolio with structured exposure to real projects and institutions.

FUTURE ENTREPRENEURS

Seeking mentorship from CAI's venture network — from idea formation through to studio and capital pathways.
